📚 Explain (ELI10)

What is The Agency? A collection of 226 specialized AI agents, each with unique personality and expertise. Think of it as assembling your dream team - these AI specialists never sleep, never complain, and always deliver.

How do I use it? Install to your AI tool (Claude Code, Cursor, etc.) via the install script, then activate any agent by asking your AI assistant to "activate [Agent Name] mode".

Why different agents? Generic AI prompts give generic results. These agents are battle-tested specialists with proven workflows, personality, and real deliverables.
