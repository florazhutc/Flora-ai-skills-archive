📚 Explain (ELI10)

What is GBrain? It gives your AI agent a personal memory. The agent ingests meetings, emails, tweets, and ideas while you sleep. It enriches every person and company it encounters.

How does it work? Every time you interact with the agent, it captures entities and creates typed links (attended, works_at, invested_in, founded) automatically. No LLM calls needed.

Why graph matters? Vector search returns semantically similar chunks. Graph returns factually connected chunks. Hybrid search pulls from both, giving +31.4 P@5 lift over vector-only RAG.

What runs overnight? The dream cycle: dedup pages, fix citations, score salience, find contradictions, prep tomorrow's tasks. You wake up smarter than when you went to bed.
